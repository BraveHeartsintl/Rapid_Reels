## 1. Why This Policy Exists

Photographs of identifiable individuals are personal data under the **UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)**. Some photographs may also contain special category data (such as religious imagery, health indicators, or biometric features).

This Policy explains the responsibilities of Users when booking photography, the consents required from subjects who will be photographed, and the rules that govern how photographs are taken, used and shared via Rapid Reels App.

This Policy forms part of our **Terms and Conditions**.

## 2. User's Warranty and Responsibility

By confirming a Booking, you warrant and confirm to Brave Hearts International that:

- You are authorised to organise photography at the Event location;
- You have obtained, or will obtain prior to or at the Event, the consent of every identifiable adult who will be photographed;
- In respect of any child being photographed, you either hold parental responsibility for that child or have obtained the verifiable consent of the parent or legal guardian;
- You have informed all subjects about the purpose of the photography, who will receive the photographs, and how they may exercise their data protection rights;
- You will not request or use photographs in a way that breaches the privacy or dignity of any subject;
- You indemnify Brave Hearts International, the Photographer, and the Editor against any claim arising from a failure to obtain such consents or authorisations.

## 3. Photographing Children

We require particular care when photographing children under the age of 18:

- Parental or guardian consent must be obtained in advance and recorded — verbal consent at the Event is acceptable for a private function, but a written record is strongly recommended;
- Photographs of children must not be requested or used in any sexualised, exploitative or otherwise harmful manner;
- Photographs of children must not be shared with anyone other than the parent or legal guardian without their further consent;
- Schools, nurseries and other settings have their own safeguarding rules — you must comply with any restrictions imposed by such settings;
- We reserve the right to refuse or terminate a Booking where we have reasonable grounds to believe child safety may be at risk.

## 4. Photographing Third Parties at Events

Events such as weddings, birthday parties and corporate gatherings typically involve guests and other third parties. We expect Users to:

- Display a clear notice at the Event (or include in the invitation) that photography will take place, who is responsible for it, and how to opt out;
- Provide a means by which any guest can decline to be photographed (for example, a designated "no photos" badge, table or area);
- Instruct the Photographer to respect any opt-out request;
- Where group photographs are taken, ensure that participation is voluntary and that any guest may step out without consequence.

A model notice you can display at the Event is available in **section 11** of this Policy.

## 5. Photography in Public and Private Spaces

- **Private venues:** Permission of the venue owner or manager is required. You confirm at booking that such permission has been obtained.
- **Public spaces:** Photography in public is generally lawful in the UK, but reasonable expectations of privacy still apply. The Photographer will use professional judgement to avoid intrusion.
- **Restricted locations:** Some sites (such as museums, places of worship, government property, transport hubs, and certain retail premises) prohibit or restrict photography. You are responsible for ensuring any necessary permits are obtained.
- **Drones and aerial photography:** Where any drone or aerial photography is to be used, all Civil Aviation Authority rules and Drone Code requirements must be complied with. Please flag this to us at the time of booking so the correct licensing and insurance can be arranged.
