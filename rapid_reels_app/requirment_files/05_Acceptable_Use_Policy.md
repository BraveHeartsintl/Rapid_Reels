## 1. Introduction

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") sets out the rules of conduct for using the Rapid Reels App application and Service. It forms part of our **Terms and Conditions**. By using the Service you agree to comply with this AUP at all times.

Breach of this AUP may result in suspension or termination of your account, cancellation of pending Bookings, and where appropriate, referral to law enforcement.

## 2. Permitted Use

You may use the Service only for the lawful purpose of:

- Browsing and discovering photography services;
- Booking Photographers for legitimate Events;
- Receiving Final Photographs and managing your account;
- Communicating respectfully with Photographers, Editors and our support staff; and
- Other purposes expressly permitted within the Service.

## 3. Prohibited Use

You must not, and must not permit any third party to:

### 3.1 Unlawful Activity

- Use the Service in any way that breaches any applicable local, national or international law or regulation;
- Book photography for any unlawful purpose, including activities that infringe the rights of any third party;
- Engage in fraud, money laundering, or financing of terrorism;
- Misrepresent your identity or affiliation with any person or organisation.

### 3.2 Harmful or Offensive Content and Conduct

- Request photography of any content that is obscene, indecent, defamatory, harassing, hateful, discriminatory, or otherwise offensive;
- Request indecent or sexualised photography of any minor, or of any adult who has not provided informed consent;
- Send abusive, threatening or harassing communications to Photographers, Editors or our staff;
- Discriminate against any Photographer, Editor or member of staff on the basis of race, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, disability, age or other characteristic protected under the **Equality Act 2010**.

### 3.3 Misuse of Photographs

- Use any Final Photograph for commercial purposes without obtaining a separate commercial licence;
- Modify, remove or obscure any watermark, signature, copyright notice or metadata, except where you have been expressly authorised;
- Share, distribute or publish any photograph that includes an identifiable third party who has not consented to such publication;
- Upload, share or publish any photograph in a way that brings the Photographer, Editor or Rapid Reels App into disrepute;
- Use any photograph to harass, defame, blackmail, or impersonate any person.

### 3.4 Technical Misuse

- Attempt to gain unauthorised access to any part of the Service, our systems or any user's account;
- Introduce malware, viruses, worms, trojans, logic bombs or other harmful material;
- Use any automated system, robot, spider or scraper to access or extract data from the Service;
- Reverse-engineer, decompile, disassemble or otherwise attempt to derive the source code of the App;
- Interfere with or disrupt the integrity, performance or availability of the Service;
- Circumvent or attempt to circumvent any security, authentication, rate limiting, or access control mechanism.

### 3.5 Account Misuse

- Create more than one account without our prior written consent;
- Share your account credentials with any other person;
- Use another person's account without authorisation;
- Provide false or misleading information during registration or at any point afterwards.

### 3.6 Booking and Payment Misuse

- Use stolen, fraudulent or unauthorised payment methods;
- Issue unwarranted chargebacks without first attempting to resolve the matter with our customer support;
- Make a Booking with the intention of cancelling, in order to disrupt the Service or a Photographer's availability;
- Attempt to circumvent our pricing, fees or commission structure (including by inducing a Photographer to take work outside the platform after introduction through the Service).

## 4. Working with Photographers and Editors

To ensure the Service runs smoothly:

- Provide clear, accurate and lawful instructions regarding the scope of work;
- Provide safe access to the Event venue and reasonable working conditions;
- Respect the professional judgement of the Photographer and Editor;
- Do not solicit Photographers or Editors to perform off-platform work that bypasses Rapid Reels App;
- Do not request edits or content that would breach this AUP.

## 5. User-Generated Content

If you upload, post or transmit any content via the Service (including reviews, photographs, messages or feedback), you confirm that:

- You own or have all necessary rights to share that content;
- The content is accurate, lawful and does not infringe any third party's rights;
- The content does not contain malicious code or unlawful material; and
- You grant us a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use, host, store, reproduce, modify and display that content for the purpose of operating and improving the Service.

We reserve the right to review, moderate or remove any user-generated content that breaches this AUP or that we consider, in our reasonable discretion, to be inappropriate.

## 6. Reporting Misuse

If you become aware of any breach of this AUP, including abuse, illegal content, or misuse of photographs, please report it immediately to **[SUPPORT_EMAIL]** with as much detail as possible. We will investigate all reports promptly and take appropriate action.

## 7. Consequences of Breach

Where we identify a breach of this AUP, we may, at our reasonable discretion and depending on the seriousness of the breach:

- Issue a warning;
- Suspend or terminate your account;
- Cancel pending Bookings without refund where the breach is material;
- Withhold the delivery of Final Photographs pending investigation;
- Pursue legal action or claim damages;
- Report the matter to the relevant law enforcement, regulatory or safeguarding authority;
- Disclose your information as required by law or as necessary to protect the rights of others.

## 8. Compliance with Online Safety Obligations

We operate the Service in line with the **Online Safety Act 2023** and relevant codes of practice. We actively work to prevent illegal content and to keep the Service safe for users of all ages. We cooperate fully with Ofcom and law enforcement where required.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this AUP from time to time. Material changes will be notified to you via the App or by email at least 30 days before they take effect. Continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the revised AUP.
