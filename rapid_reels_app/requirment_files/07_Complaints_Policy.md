## 1. Our Commitment

At Brave Hearts International we are committed to providing a high-quality service. We recognise that sometimes things do not go as planned, and we take all complaints seriously. This Policy explains how to raise a complaint and what you can expect from us when you do.

This Policy works alongside, and does not affect, your statutory rights under UK consumer law, including the **Consumer Rights Act 2015** and the **Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013**.

## 2. Scope

This Policy covers complaints relating to:

- The conduct or performance of a Photographer or Editor;
- The quality of Final Photographs delivered;
- Booking, payment, refund or cancellation issues;
- Our handling of your personal data;
- Any other aspect of the Rapid Reels App Service.

## 4. Our Complaint Handling Timeline

### 4.1 Acknowledgement

We will acknowledge your formal complaint within **3 working days** of receipt and provide you with a unique complaint reference number and the name of the person handling your case.

### 4.2 Investigation

We will investigate your complaint fairly, impartially and thoroughly. The Complaints Manager will:

- Review all information you have provided;
- Speak with relevant staff, Photographers or Editors involved;
- Examine our records, communications and any photographs at issue;
- Consider what went wrong and what should be done to put things right.

### 4.3 Substantive Response

We will provide you with a full written response within **14 working days** of acknowledgement. This response will set out:

- The outcome of our investigation;
- Our findings on each issue raised;
- Any remedy we are offering (such as a refund, repeat performance, price reduction or goodwill gesture);
- The next steps if you are not satisfied with our response.

If we need longer than 14 working days due to the complexity of the matter, we will write to you to explain the reason for the delay and the revised timeline. In any event, we will aim to resolve all complaints within **8 weeks** of receipt.

## 5. Escalation

If you are not satisfied with our substantive response, you may request that your complaint be escalated by replying to the response within **14 days**, stating clearly which aspects you remain dissatisfied with.

An escalated complaint will be reviewed by a senior member of staff (or a Director) who has not been previously involved. A final response will be provided within a further **14 working days**.

## 6. Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR)

If your complaint remains unresolved after our internal complaints process has concluded (or after 8 weeks, whichever is earlier), you may refer the matter to an approved Alternative Dispute Resolution provider. ADR offers an independent, less formal and less costly alternative to court proceedings.

## 7. The Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)

If your complaint concerns the handling of your personal data and you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**:

- ICO Helpline: **0303 123 1113**
- Website: **https://ico.org.uk**
- Address: Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF

We would, however, appreciate the opportunity to address your concerns before you contact the ICO.

## 8. Your Right to Bring Court Proceedings

Nothing in this Policy prevents you from pursuing your complaint through the courts. Where appropriate, small claims (under £10,000 in England and Wales) may be brought through the Money Claim Online service: **https://www.gov.uk/make-court-claim-for-money**.

## 9. Vexatious or Repeated Complaints

We will treat all complaints seriously. However, where a complaint is repeated and has already been responded to, or where the conduct of the complainant is abusive, threatening or unreasonable, we reserve the right to limit further engagement, while remaining willing to consider any new and material information.

## 10. Confidentiality and Data Protection

All complaints will be handled confidentially and in accordance with our **Privacy Policy**. Information about your complaint will only be shared with those who need it to investigate and resolve the matter. Records of complaints are retained for **6 years** to satisfy our regulatory and audit obligations.

## 11. Continuous Improvement

We monitor complaints to identify trends and improve our service. Anonymised data may be reviewed by our management team, used in staff training, and shared with our insurers and regulators where appropriate.
