## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website or use a mobile application. They allow the site or app to remember your actions, preferences, and information about your visit, so you do not have to re-enter them each time.

We also use other similar technologies including pixels, software development kit (SDK) identifiers, and local storage. Throughout this Policy, the term "cookies" refers to all such technologies.

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Service to function and cannot be switched off. They are usually only set in response to actions made by you which amount to a request for a service, such as logging in, securing your account, or processing a Booking. We do not require your consent to use these cookies, but you can block them via your browser settings — though some parts of the Service may then not work.

- Authentication and session management;
- Security and fraud prevention;
- Load balancing and service routing;
- Recording your cookie preferences.

### 3.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our Service. They help us understand which pages and features are most popular, and how visitors move around the App. **We only use these cookies with your consent.**

- Page views, session duration, and feature usage;
- Crash reports and error logs;
- Aggregated user journey data.

### 3.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable the Service to provide enhanced functionality and personalisation. They remember your preferences (such as language, location, or chosen photographer style). **We only use these cookies with your consent.**

### 3.4 Marketing and Advertising Cookies

These cookies may be set by us or by third-party advertising partners. They are used to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant adverts on other sites, and to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. **We only use these cookies with your explicit consent.**

## 5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

On **Android** and our **website**, when you first use the Service you may see a cookie consent banner that allows you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. The **iOS / iPadOS** app does not show this banner (see section 1.1).

You can change your preferences at any time by:

- Tapping **"Cookie Preferences"** within the App settings (Android only);
- Visiting the cookie preferences link in the footer of our website;
- Adjusting your browser settings to block or delete cookies;
- Using device-level controls (e.g. "Limit ad tracking" on iOS or "Opt out of ads personalisation" on Android).

Withdrawing or refusing cookies may affect the functionality of the Service. Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled.

## 6. How Long Cookies Last

- **Session cookies** are temporary and are deleted when you close your browser or exit the App.
- **Persistent cookies** remain on your device for a set period (up to 24 months) or until you delete them.
