## 3. The Personal Data We Collect

### 3.1 Information You Provide to Us

- **Identity data:** full name, date of birth, gender (where you choose to provide it).
- **Contact data:** billing address, event address, email address, telephone number.
- **Account data:** username, password (stored in hashed form), profile picture, preferences.
- **Booking data:** event type, date, time, location, guest count, special requirements, photography preferences.
- **Payment data:** transaction history, last four digits of payment card, billing details. Full card details are processed directly by our payment provider and are not stored on our systems.
- **Communications data:** messages exchanged through the App, support tickets, feedback and reviews.

### 3.2 Image Data (Special Category Where Applicable)

- **Photographs taken at your Event**, which may depict you, your guests, and other identifiable individuals. Photographs are considered personal data under the UK GDPR.
- Photographs may, in some circumstances, contain **special category data** (for example, where they reveal religious or philosophical beliefs in a religious ceremony, health information, or biometric features). We process this data only with explicit consent and on the additional legal basis described in section 5.

### 3.3 Information We Collect Automatically

- **Device data:** device type, operating system, unique device identifier, mobile network information.
- **Usage data:** pages and screens viewed, features used, time spent in the App, crash reports.
- **Location data:** approximate location based on IP address, and precise location only if you grant permission (used to suggest local photographers).
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** see our Cookie Policy for full details.

### 3.4 Information from Third Parties

- **Authentication providers** (e.g. Google, Apple, Facebook) if you sign in via social login.
- **Payment processors** (transaction confirmation and fraud signals).
- **Analytics providers** (aggregated usage data, where you have given consent).

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the UK GDPR, we rely on the following legal bases to process your personal data:

- **Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)):** to fulfil our obligations under our Terms and Conditions, including processing your Bookings and delivering photographs.
- **Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)):** to operate, secure and improve the Service; to prevent fraud; to send service-related communications; and to enforce our legal rights. We balance our interests against your fundamental rights and only rely on this basis where appropriate.
- **Consent (Article 6(1)(a) and Article 9(2)(a) for special category data):** to send marketing communications; to use precise location data; to use your photographs for our marketing or portfolio; and to process special category data within photographs. You may withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)):** to comply with tax laws, anti-money laundering laws, court orders and other legal requirements.

## 6. Children's Data

The Service is intended for users aged 18 and over. However, photographs of children may be captured at Events (such as birthday parties or family gatherings) lawfully booked by an adult.

Where photographs of a child are taken, the User confirms at the point of booking that they hold parental responsibility for the child, or have obtained the verifiable consent of the parent or legal guardian of any other child being photographed, in line with our Image Rights and Subject Consent Policy.

We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such data, we will delete it promptly.
