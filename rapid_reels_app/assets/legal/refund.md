## 1. Introduction

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains your rights and our procedures when you wish to cancel a Booking with Rapid Reels App, or when we are unable to deliver a Booking. This Policy forms part of our **Terms and Conditions** and applies to all Bookings made through the Rapid Reels App application.

This Policy reflects your statutory rights under the **Consumer Rights Act 2015** and the **Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013**. Nothing in this Policy limits or excludes those statutory rights.

## 2. Your 14-Day Cooling-Off Right

As a consumer entering into a contract for services at a distance (through the App), you have the right to cancel within **14 days** of confirming a Booking, without giving any reason. This is known as the cooling-off period.

### 2.1 Important Exception

Where you ask us to begin performing the service (for example, dispatching a Photographer to your Event) before the end of the 14-day cooling-off period, and the service is then fully performed within that period, **you lose your right to cancel** under this section.

Where the service has been partially performed during the cooling-off period and you then cancel, you must pay an amount proportionate to the work completed up to the point of cancellation.

If your Event falls within 14 days of the Booking date, by confirming the Booking you expressly request that we begin performance immediately and acknowledge that you will lose your statutory right of cancellation once the service is fully performed.

### 2.2 How to Exercise Your Right to Cancel

To cancel within the cooling-off period, you must inform us of your decision by a clear statement. You may use any of the following methods:

- Tap **"Cancel Booking"** within the App;
- Email **[SUPPORT_EMAIL]** with the subject line "Cancellation – [Booking Reference]";
- Write to us at [REGISTERED_ADDRESS].

To meet the cancellation deadline, it is sufficient that you send your communication concerning the exercise of the right to cancel before the 14-day period has expired.

In rare circumstances we may need to cancel a Booking, for example where:

- There are safety concerns at the proposed Event location;
- There has been a breach of these Terms or our Acceptable Use Policy;
- Payment cannot be processed; or
- Continuing with the Booking would be unlawful.

If a Photographer fails to attend your Event (and we have not informed you of a substitute in advance), you will be entitled to a **full refund** of the Service Fee. We may, in addition and at our discretion, offer a goodwill credit or a discount on a future Booking.

### 7.1 Right to Repeat Performance

You may request that we, at our cost and within a reasonable time, repeat the relevant elements of the service. In the context of photography, this typically means re-editing the affected images, or where appropriate and reasonably practicable, arranging a re-shoot.

### 7.2 Right to a Price Reduction

Where repeat performance is impossible or would cause significant inconvenience, you are entitled to a price reduction of an appropriate amount, up to a full refund.
